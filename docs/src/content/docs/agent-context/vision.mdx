## Product identity

**Crosswalker supports acquiring, shaping, connecting, understanding, sharing, and maintaining ontology-shaped knowledge over time.**

The product is an [ontology lifecycle](/crosswalker/concepts/ontology-lifecycle/) system, not only an importer or Recipe runner — in one sentence, a structural [reification](/crosswalker/concepts/terminology/#reification) and ontology lifecycle management engine. Import is the entry point; durable value comes from keeping structured knowledge navigable, connected, explainable, portable, and maintainable as sources and local needs evolve.

## Operating model

**Crosswalker manages schema-governed Knowledge Sets using machine-evaluable declarative Recipes.**

| Role | Meaning |
|---|---|
| [Schema Contract](/crosswalker/concepts/artifact-roles-and-authority/#schema-contract-governing-meta-artifact) | Governs which representations are valid |
| [Knowledge Set](/crosswalker/concepts/artifact-roles-and-authority/#knowledge-set-what-is-asserted) | Extensional family: the concepts and assertions being managed |
| [Recipe](/crosswalker/concepts/artifact-roles-and-authority/#recipe-what-should-be-done) | Intensional family: portable intended behavior |
| Tier 1 | Canonical Crosswalker-managed representation and local authority, not a root artifact family |

The system's deeper mechanism is [data morphology](/crosswalker/agent-context/zz-log/2026-06-04-data-morphology/): decode and re-embody source shapes on the write side; project and present useful shapes on the read side.

## Machine-actionability

**Crosswalker makes ontology knowledge machine-actionable through portable recipes, schemas, queries, and mappings.**

- **Portable Recipes** make repeatable behavior inspectable, validatable, shareable, and replayable.
- **Schema Contracts** let the plugin, external tools, scripts, agents, and future runtimes produce or consume conforming artifacts.
- **Queries** turn governed knowledge into answers and views without replacing canonical Tier 1 authority.
- **Mappings** connect concepts across Knowledge Sets while preserving typed assertions and provenance.

## Current and planned

| Horizon | Capabilities |
|---|---|
| **Current** | CSV/XLSX/JSON import; visual mapping and preview; schema-validated Tier 1 generation; portable ImportRecipe preservation; typed crosswalk records; Bases query surfaces; first export paths |
| **Next** | Durable local Recipe library and browser; full-source portability proofs; first-class MappingSet identity and authoring; broader export/share workflows |
| **Later** | Ontology evolution and migration; separate Execution Record and Package Manifest contracts; stronger audit, packaging, and community distribution |

Implementation status lives in the [milestone roadmap](/crosswalker/reference/roadmap/milestones/). Artifact roles and authority live in the [permanent concept model](/crosswalker/concepts/artifact-roles-and-authority/).

## Design principles

1. **Plain text first** — canonical managed knowledge remains readable Markdown and frontmatter.
2. **Schema governed** — machine-readable contracts outrank any one engine or UI projection.
3. **General ontology core, compliance-first experience** — internal primitives work across domains; launch workflows lead with GRC needs.
4. **Portable and replaceable** — Recipes, schemas, mappings, and queries survive changes in tools and runtimes.
5. **Retrieval centered** — structure and views help people decide what they need to know and reach it.
6. **UI first, configuration as escape hatch** — every lifecycle stage needs an understandable interface; declarative files remain the programmatic path.
7. **Authority is explicit** — authority follows ontological role, not storage location, format, lifecycle phase, or UI model.
8. **No artifact-noun inflation** — new root nouns require genuinely independent identity, lifecycle, authority, and consumers.
